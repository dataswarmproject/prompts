# AI Glossary

**Curator: Dr. Ahmed Halloub**

Essential AI terminology explained simply for professionals.

---

## A

**Agent**
An AI system that can take actions to achieve goals, often using tools and multiple steps.

**API (Application Programming Interface)**
A way for programs to communicate with AI services programmatically.

**Anthropic**
Company behind Claude AI models.

---

## B

**Batch Processing**
Sending multiple requests together, often at lower cost than real-time.

**Benchmark**
Standardized test to measure AI performance.

**BERT**
Bidirectional Encoder Representations from Transformers - older but influential language model.

---

## C

**Chain-of-Thought**
Prompting technique where AI shows reasoning steps.

**ChatGPT**
OpenAI's conversational AI interface.

**Claude**
Anthropic's family of AI models (Opus, Sonnet, Haiku).

**Context Window**
Maximum amount of text an AI can process at once (measured in tokens).

**Completion**
AI-generated response to a prompt.

---

## D

**DALL-E**
OpenAI's image generation model.

**Dataset**
Collection of data used for training or testing AI models.

**Diffusion Model**
Type of AI used for image generation (like Stable Diffusion).

---

## E

**Embedding**
Numerical representation of text/data that captures semantic meaning.

**Epoch**
One complete pass through training data during model training.

---

## F

**Few-Shot Learning**
Providing examples in prompt to guide AI behavior.

**Fine-Tuning**
Training a pre-existing model on specific data for specialized tasks.

**Function Calling**
AI's ability to call external functions/APIs with structured parameters.

---

## G

**Gemini**
Google's family of AI models (Flash, Pro, Ultra).

**GPT**
Generative Pre-trained Transformer - OpenAI's model family.

**GPU**
Graphics Processing Unit - hardware used for AI training and inference.

---

## H

**Hallucination**
When AI generates false or nonsensical information confidently.

**HumanEval**
Benchmark for evaluating code generation capabilities.

**Hyperparameter**
Setting that controls model training behavior (like learning rate).

---

## I

**Inference**
Process of using a trained model to generate outputs.

**Instruction Following**
AI's ability to follow specific directions in prompts.

---

## J

**Jailbreak**
Attempt to bypass AI safety restrictions.

**JSON Mode**
AI output formatted as structured JSON data.

---

## K

**k-shot**
Providing k examples in a prompt (e.g., 3-shot = 3 examples).

---

## L

**LangChain**
Popular framework for building AI applications with LLMs.

**Latency**
Time delay between request and response.

**LLM (Large Language Model)**
AI model trained on vast text to understand and generate language.

**LlamaIndex**
Framework focused on data indexing and retrieval for LLMs.

---

## M

**MCP (Model Context Protocol)**
Standard for connecting AI models to external data sources and tools.

**Midjourney**
Popular AI image generation service.

**MMLU**
Massive Multitask Language Understanding - benchmark testing knowledge across 57 subjects.

---

## N

**Neural Network**
Computing system inspired by biological brains, basis of modern AI.

**NLP (Natural Language Processing)**
Field of AI focused on understanding and generating human language.

---

## O

**One-Shot**
Providing single example in prompt.

**OpenAI**
Company behind GPT models and ChatGPT.

---

## P

**Parameter**
Individual weight in neural network; more parameters generally means more capable model.

**Perplexity**
AI-powered search engine that cites sources.

**Pinecone**
Popular cloud vector database.

**Prompt**
Input text given to AI to generate response.

**Prompt Engineering**
Art and science of crafting effective prompts.

---

## Q

**Quantization**
Reducing model size by using lower precision numbers, enabling faster/cheaper inference.

**Query**
Question or request sent to AI or database.

---

## R

**RAG (Retrieval Augmented Generation)**
Technique combining search with AI generation for more accurate, sourced responses.

**Rate Limit**
Maximum number of requests allowed in a time period.

**RLHF (Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback)**
Training method using human preferences to improve AI behavior.

---

## S

**Semantic Search**
Search based on meaning rather than exact keywords.

**Stable Diffusion**
Open-source image generation model.

**System Prompt**
Instructions that set AI's behavior and role.

---

## T

**Temperature**
Setting controlling randomness in AI outputs (0 = deterministic, 2 = very random).

**Token**
Basic unit of text for AI models (~4 characters or 0.75 words).

**TPM (Tokens Per Minute)**
Rate limit based on token usage.

**Transformer**
Neural network architecture underlying most modern LLMs.

---

## U

**Upstream Task**
Task model was originally trained on before fine-tuning.

---

## V

**Vector**
Numerical array representing data in multi-dimensional space.

**Vector Database**
Database optimized for storing and searching vectors/embeddings.

**Vision Model**
AI that can understand and analyze images.

---

## W

**Whisper**
OpenAI's speech recognition model.

**Word Embedding**
Vector representation of a word capturing its meaning.

---

## Z

**Zero-Shot**
AI performing task without examples, using only instruction.

---

## Common Metrics

**MTEB Score**
Massive Text Embedding Benchmark - measures embedding model quality.

**Pass@k**
Percentage of problems solved when model generates k solutions.

**BLEU Score**
Measures translation/generation quality against references.

**F1 Score**
Measure of classification accuracy balancing precision and recall.
