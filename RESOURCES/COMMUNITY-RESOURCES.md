## Community Guidelines

### How to Get the Most from Communities

**Do:**
- Search before asking
- Provide context and details
- Share your solutions
- Be respectful and professional
- Contribute back to discussions

**Don't:**
- Ask for complete solutions without effort
- Spam with promotional content
- Share API keys or credentials
- Post the same question everywhere
- Ignore community guidelines
