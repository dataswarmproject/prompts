## Strategy and Planning

### Strategic Plan

```
Develop a strategic plan for [company/initiative]

Company context:
- Industry: [industry]
- Size: [employees, revenue]
- Current position: [market position]
- Stage: [startup/growth/mature]

Strategic challenge:
[Describe current situation and what needs addressing]

Time horizon: [1-year/3-year/5-year]

Create:
1. Situation Analysis:
   - Strengths
   - Weaknesses
   - Opportunities
   - Threats
   - Key trends affecting us

2. Strategic Vision:
   - Where we want to be
   - What success looks like
   - Market position goal

3. Strategic Priorities (3-5):
   - Priority 1: [what]
     - Why important
     - Key initiatives
     - Success metrics
     - Resources needed
   - [Repeat for each priority]

4. Implementation Roadmap:
   - Phase 1 (months 1-X): [focus]
   - Phase 2 (months X-Y): [focus]
   - Phase 3 (months Y-Z): [focus]

5. Success Metrics:
   - Leading indicators
   - Lagging indicators
   - Monitoring cadence

6. Risks and Mitigation:
   - Top 5 risks
   - Mitigation strategy for each

Format: Executive-friendly, actionable
```

### Competitive Analysis

```
Conduct a comprehensive competitive analysis for [company]

Our company:
- Product/Service: [description]
- Target market: [description]
- Unique value: [differentiation]

Competitors to analyze:
1. [Competitor 1]: [main competitor]
2. [Competitor 2]: [similar model]
3. [Competitor 3]: [disruptor]
4. [Competitor 4]: [traditional player]

Analysis framework:
1. For each competitor:
   - Business model
   - Revenue model
   - Pricing strategy
   - Target customers
   - Market share (estimate)
   - Strengths
   - Weaknesses
   - Recent moves

2. Competitive positioning matrix:
   - Map competitors on [dimension 1] vs [dimension 2]
   - Identify white space

3. Competitive advantages:
   - What they do better than us
   - What we do better than them

4. Threat assessment:
   - Who's the biggest threat? Why?
   - Emerging competitors

5. Strategic recommendations:
   - How to differentiate
   - Where to compete
   - Where to avoid

6. Monitoring plan:
   - What to track
   - How often
   - Tools to use

Include: Comparison table, positioning map, threat matrix
```

## Business Plans

### Lean Business Plan

```
Create a lean business plan for [business idea]

Business concept:
- Problem: [what problem are you solving]
- Solution: [your product/service]
- Target customer: [who specifically]

Current stage: [idea/prototype/early revenue]

Provide concise sections:

1. Executive Summary (1 paragraph):
   - What, who, why, how much

2. Problem & Solution:
   - Customer pain points (top 3)
   - How your solution addresses each
   - Unique value proposition (1 sentence)

3. Market Opportunity:
   - Market size (TAM, SAM, SOM)
   - Market trends
   - Target segment

4. Business Model:
   - Revenue streams
   - Pricing strategy
   - Unit economics
   - Key metrics

5. Competition:
   - Direct competitors
   - Indirect alternatives
   - Competitive advantage

6. Go-to-Market:
   - Customer acquisition strategy
   - Sales process
   - Marketing channels
   - CAC and LTV estimates

7. Operations:
   - Key activities
   - Key resources
   - Key partners

8. Financial Projections (3 years):
   - Revenue projection
   - Key expenses
   - Break-even point
   - Funding needs

9. Team:
   - Key roles needed
   - Current team strengths
   - Gaps to fill

10. Milestones:
    - Next 6 months
    - Next 12 months
    - 3-year vision

Format: 3-5 pages, suitable for investors/partners
```

### Pitch Deck Content

```
Create content for a [seed/Series A/Series B] pitch deck

Company: [name and description]
Problem: [problem you're solving]
Solution: [your solution]
Traction: [current metrics]

Create content for these slides:

1. Hook (opening line):
   - Grab attention
   - Set the stage

2. Problem (the pain):
   - Who experiences it
   - How big is it
   - Why now

3. Solution (your product):
   - What it is
   - How it works
   - Why it's 10x better

4. Product Demo (key features):
   - [Feature 1 and benefit]
   - [Feature 2 and benefit]
   - [Feature 3 and benefit]

5. Market Opportunity:
   - Market size
   - Growth rate
   - Your slice

6. Business Model:
   - How you make money
   - Pricing
   - Unit economics

7. Traction:
   - Key metrics
   - Growth rate
   - Milestones achieved

8. Competition:
   - Competitive landscape
   - Positioning
   - Unfair advantage

9. Go-to-Market:
   - Distribution strategy
   - Customer acquisition
   - Partnerships

10. Team:
    - Why this team
    - Relevant experience
    - Advisors

11. Financials:
    - Revenue projection
    - Key assumptions
    - Path to profitability

12. Ask:
    - Amount raising
    - Use of funds
    - Runway

13. Vision (closer):
    - Long-term vision
    - Impact
    - Call to action

For each slide provide:
- Headline (key message)
- Bullet points (3-5 max)
- Visual suggestions
- Talking points for presenter

Tone: Confident, ambitious, backed by data
```

## Market Research

### Customer Research Plan

```
Design a customer research plan for [product/market]

Research goals:
- [Goal 1: e.g., understand buying behavior]
- [Goal 2: e.g., identify pain points]
- [Goal 3: e.g., test feature concepts]

Target segments:
- [Segment 1]: [description]
- [Segment 2]: [description]

Create:
1. Research questions (prioritized):
   - [Question 1]
   - [Question 2]
   - [Question 3]

2. Research methods:
   - Method 1: [interviews/surveys/observation]
     - Sample size
     - Recruitment criteria
     - Timeline
   - Method 2: [...]

3. Interview guide (if applicable):
   - Introduction script
   - Warm-up questions
   - Core questions
   - Probing questions
   - Wrap-up

4. Survey design (if applicable):
   - Question types
   - Question flow
   - Length estimate
   - Distribution plan

5. Analysis framework:
   - How to code responses
   - Patterns to look for
   - Reporting format

6. Success criteria:
   - What good looks like
   - Minimum viable insights

Budget: [if applicable]
Timeline: [duration]
```

### Market Sizing

```
Estimate the market size for [product/service] in [geography]

Product: [description]
Target customer: [specific segment]

Calculate:
1. TAM (Total Addressable Market):
   - Top-down approach
   - Bottom-up approach
   - Show assumptions

2. SAM (Serviceable Available Market):
   - Geographic constraints
   - Segment focus
   - Practical limitations

3. SOM (Serviceable Obtainable Market):
   - Year 1
   - Year 3
   - Year 5
   - Market share assumptions

Provide:
- Calculation methodology
- Data sources used
- Key assumptions
- Confidence level
- Comparable markets
- Sensitivity analysis

Format:
- Executive summary
- Detailed calculations
- Visual representation (describe)
- Appendix with sources

Make assumptions explicit and realistic
```

## Financial Analysis

### Financial Model

```
Build a financial model for [business/project]

Business type: [SaaS/ecommerce/marketplace/etc.]
Stage: [startup/growth/expansion]
Time horizon: [months/years to model]

Revenue assumptions:
- [Revenue stream 1]: [assumptions]
- [Revenue stream 2]: [assumptions]

Cost structure:
- Fixed costs: [major categories]
- Variable costs: [cost per unit/customer]

Create model with:
1. Revenue projections:
   - Month-by-month (Year 1)
   - Quarterly (Years 2-3)
   - Key drivers and growth rates

2. Cost projections:
   - COGS
   - Operating expenses
   - Headcount plan
   - Marketing spend

3. Key metrics:
   - Gross margin
   - Contribution margin
   - Break-even point
   - Cash flow
   - Burn rate
   - Runway

4. Scenarios:
   - Base case
   - Best case
   - Worst case

5. Assumptions table:
   - All key assumptions listed
   - Sources/rationale

6. Funding requirements:
   - When needed
   - How much
   - Use of funds

Provide:
- Summary P&L
- Cash flow statement
- Key metric dashboard
- Formula explanations

Tool: [Excel/Google Sheets/preferred]
```

### ROI Analysis

```
Analyze the ROI for [investment/initiative]

Investment:
- Initiative: [description]
- Total cost: [amount]
- Time period: [duration]
- Cost breakdown:
  - [Category 1]: [amount]
  - [Category 2]: [amount]

Expected benefits:
- [Benefit 1]: [quantifiable]
- [Benefit 2]: [quantifiable]
- [Benefit 3]: [quantifiable]

Calculate:
1. Financial ROI:
   - Total costs
   - Total benefits (monetized)
   - Net benefit
   - ROI percentage
   - Payback period
   - NPV (if multi-year)

2. Risk assessment:
   - Implementation risks
   - Adoption risks
   - Market risks
   - Probability-adjusted ROI

3. Sensitivity analysis:
   - Best case scenario
   - Expected case
   - Worst case scenario

4. Intangible benefits:
   - [Benefit 1: hard to quantify]
   - [Benefit 2: strategic value]

5. Recommendation:
   - Go/No-go with rationale
   - Conditions for success
   - Alternatives considered

6. Monitoring plan:
   - KPIs to track
   - Review schedule
   - Decision points

Format: Executive summary with detailed appendix
```

## Operations

### Process Improvement

```
Optimize this business process:

Current process: [process name]
Purpose: [what it accomplishes]

Current state:
- Steps: [list current steps]
- Time: [current cycle time]
- Resources: [people/tools involved]
- Pain points:
  - [Issue 1]
  - [Issue 2]
  - [Issue 3]

Volume: [frequency/throughput]
Stakeholders: [who's involved]

Analyze and provide:
1. Process map:
   - Current state (visual description)
   - Bottlenecks identified
   - Waste identified

2. Root cause analysis:
   - Why inefficiencies exist
   - Contributing factors

3. Improved process:
   - Future state process map
   - Specific changes
   - Steps eliminated/added/modified

4. Impact analysis:
   - Time savings
   - Cost reduction
   - Quality improvement
   - Resource efficiency

5. Implementation plan:
   - Phase 1: [quick wins]
   - Phase 2: [major changes]
   - Phase 3: [optimization]

6. Change management:
   - Training needs
   - Communication plan
   - Resistance management

7. Metrics:
   - How to measure success
   - Baseline vs. target

Tools/methods: [Lean/Six Sigma/Agile/etc.]
```

## Management

### OKR Framework

```
Create OKRs for [team/department/company]

Context:
- Organization: [size, industry]
- Level: [company/department/team]
- Time period: [quarter/year]
- Strategic priorities: [list priorities]

Develop:

Objective 1: [Inspiring, qualitative goal]

Key Results:
- KR1: [Measurable outcome from X to Y]
- KR2: [Measurable outcome from X to Y]
- KR3: [Measurable outcome from X to Y]

Initiatives:
- [How we'll achieve KR1]
- [How we'll achieve KR2]
- [How we'll achieve KR3]

[Repeat for 3-5 objectives]

For each OKR provide:
- Why this matters (alignment to strategy)
- Owner
- Dependencies
- Confidence level
- Potential obstacles

Ensure:
- Objectives are ambitious but achievable
- Key Results are specific and measurable
- Mix of input and output metrics
- Alignment across levels
- Focus (not too many)

Include measurement plan and review cadence
```
